### Finding 2: RecoverVessel destroys running-lab WIP — escrowed science dies, live total regresses — CONFIRMED

**Verification**:

No finalization anywhere on the recovery path — contrast `apply_stop_research` (research.rs:1057+), which finalizes and returns the remnant to the pool, and `finalize_project` (research.rs:506-531), which banks accrual — the machinery exists and is simply not used before removal.
- `science_at` (research.rs:619-627) = `banked + Σ accrued(p)` over live projects → dropping a running project on recovery mathematically decreases the live total. Confirmed.
- Guard asymmetry confirmed: `apply_recover_data` (research.rs:843-880) explicitly rejects while "escrow inside a live project … finish the project, first" (research.rs:874-880); `apply_recover_vessel` runs no such guard. ### Finding 4: Economy imposes no cap on the contracts instant-completion faucet — CONFIRMED
**Original**: Only bound on contract credits is `funds_micros.saturating_add` (u64); a positive credit also clears `insolvent`.

**Verification**:
- crates/csp-sim/src/contracts.rs:786-816 (`AcceptContract` tail): offer removed, `ContractAdvance` credited, then `if self.objective_met(&self.contracts.active[&id].params, None) { self.complete_contract(id, shadow)?; }` — advance + completion credit in the same apply for an already-satisfied objective. `complete_contract` (contracts.rs:1002-1033) credits `rewards.completion_funds` uncapped under `FundsReason::ContractCompletion`.
- Faucet-loop premise holds: `publish_contracts` (contracts.rs:704-724) refills the board to `board_size` whenever offers drop below it, so each accept frees a slot that regenerates. Multiple objectives are state-based and repeatable at acceptance — e.g. `SurveyData` compares cumulative *returned* units (`survey_returned_units(...) >= min_units`, contracts.rs:834-838) against a monotonically growing fact, so once returned units exceed a generated offer's threshold, that offer (and every subsequent regeneration drawing a lower threshold) completes instantly at acceptance. `SatelliteOrbit`, `PartTest`, `ColonyResupply` are likewise live-state predicates.
- Economy side (this cluster's cross-check): every contract credit funnels to `credit` (crates/csp-sim/src/economy.rs:487-496): `e.funds_micros = e.funds_micros.saturating_add(micros)` is the entire bound — no per-reason cap, no per-tick income limit, no completion cooldown/counter anywhere in `EconomyState` (struct at economy.rs:226-260 area: funds, ledger, insolvent, salary cadence, sites, crew — nothing contract-counting).
- `insolvent` laundering confirmed at economy.rs:491-493: any credit that leaves funds > 0 clears the flag. Nuance: the module doc (economy.rs:71-73) explicitly documents this as intended ("the flag clears when a credit lifts funds above zero … a recovered program re-notifies on its next insolvency"), so it is designed behavior, not an accident — but combined with the uncapped faucet it does mean faucet income instantly launders the bankruptcy state, exactly as C6/report state.
