### [simple2] Finding 1: Baumgarte angular excursion unbounded by MAX_TICK_POS_CORRECTION — CONFIRMED (structural) — SEVERITY OVERTURNED (HIGH → MEDIUM; the numeric evidence is wrong by ~400×)
**Original**: The cap only scales σ vs linear travel (σ/m ≤ 0.2); angular pseudo-impulse ‖I⁻¹(r×nσ)‖ unbounded; Euler quaternion step invalid for large dθ; offset-mass craft get 0.8 rad per vertex-iteration at 5 cm bite, ~16 rad buried 1 m.
**Verification**: Structural facts all confirmed: the only budget check is `com_travel = sigma/mass` vs `remaining` (ground: `simple.rs:1827-1836`; pair: 1876-1890); the angular response `dtheta_body = c.inv_i.mul_vec(r_body.cross(n_body * sigma))` (1847, and `pair_position_correction` 960) has no clamp or angular budget; the quaternion update is the first-order Euler step `q += 0.5·(dθ⊗q)` + normalize (1849-1857, 962-970); and the doc claim that the cap "bounds the total correction" (1797-1799) is literally true only of CoM translation.
HOWEVER the numeric core of the finding is wrong: `kn = 1/K` where `K = inv_m + (I⁻¹(r×n))×r·n` (`Constraint::new`'s `effective`, `simple.rs:469-472`, mirrored in `JointConstraint` 867-871) — the angular effective-mass term is included, which the report's example ignores. Recomputed for the report's own craft (m = 20,050 kg pod+5 m boom, I = 1250 about the torque axis, r = 5 m, depth = 0.05 m): K = 1/20050 + 25/1250 = 0.02005 → kn = 49.9 (report assumed kn ≈ m = 20,000), σ = 0.5 (report: 2,000), dθ = 0.0020 rad (report: 0.8 rad) — 400× smaller, because the angular term dominates K by exactly the ratio that makes σ small when I⁻¹(r×n) is large. The true worst case over lever arm for that craft is 0.020 rad/application (at lever = radius of gyration, 0.25 m). The claim "buried deeper than 1 m, σ saturates at 0.2·m" is also false for this geometry: at 1 m burial σ = 10, com-travel 5e-4 m (cap 0.2 never engages; saturation needs ~130 m). Cap-limited dθ = 0.2·m·a/I_k is genuinely reachable in radians only for low-radius-of-gyration craft (e.g. m = 500, I = 300, a = 1 → 0.33 rad/tick) deeply buried — a real but narrow class (deep spawn-inside-terrain / hard crash), not "centimetres of bite" on offset-mass craft, and hard to build from authored parts whose inertias come from solid-box/cylinder formulas. Verdict: the missing angular budget and the invalid Euler quaternion step are real hardening gaps worth fixing (clamp ‖I⁻¹(r×n·σ)‖, exact axis-angle rotation), but "orientation corruption for offset-mass craft" as demonstrated does not follow from the code; downgrade to MEDIUM.
